# Nordwind Customer Policies

## Returns and refunds

Items can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within 5 working days. Each order can be refunded once. Items damaged in transit are always refundable within the 30-day window.

## Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are shipped. Once an order has shipped, it can't be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery.

## Shipping

Accessories are delivered in 2 to 4 working days within Germany. E-bikes are delivered in 5 to 8 working days. Every shipped order has a tracking number.

## Warranty

E-bikes have a 2-year warranty on the frame, motor and battery. Accessories have a 1-year warranty. Warranty claims are handled by the service team, not through a refund.
